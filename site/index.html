Sample data

From screenshot to verified payment

Phones collect, your server reads, a person confirms, the bank SMS settles it. Nothing is marked final until an operator has looked.

Collect

Receipts and SMS arrive on their own

The Android agent picks up new receipt images from one approved folder, and bank SMS that match your rules. Everything waits encrypted until it syncs.

Review

Fields pre-filled, people decide

Relay reads reference, amount, date and time. An operator confirms or corrects each one, and duplicates of the same reference are flagged.

Verify

The bank SMS has the last word

A receipt counts as verified only when one bank SMS on the linked phone has the same amount and minute. One SMS can never vouch for two receipts.

What every receipt ends up as

A screenshot can be edited. A bank SMS comes from the bank to your own phone. Relay shows which receipts the bank backed up and which it did not.

Nothing leaves a phone without four yeses

Collection starts only when every link holds, and the agent shows which one is missing. Data goes to your server, not ours. There is no paid cloud service behind it.

  1. The person holding the phone allows it
    A switch per collector: bank SMS, receipt images.
  2. Android allows it
    SMS permission, and access to one chosen folder only.
  3. Your administrator turns it on
    Rules on the server decide which SMS senders and folders count.
  4. The phone is enrolled
    With a one-time QR code. Disable the phone and collection stops.

Set up a phone in five minutes

For staff whose phone collects receipts or bank SMS. Your administrator shows a QR code under Devices → Link phone; the app scans it.
